What it does

Six local engines, cloning from a short sample

Higgs Audio v3, Fish Audio Drama 3, Audio8 TTS, Supertonic 3, Qwen3-TTS and Qwen3-TTS with Russian stress run on your card.

Russian that sounds right

Numbers in the right case and gender, dates, times, money and units are written out before the engine reads.

Every take checked by ear

Each take is recognised back and compared with the text word by word, with checks for clipping, silence, cut-off, a click, a repeated phrase and noise instead of speech.

Dialogs and podcasts

«Anna:» and «[Anna]» lines become characters with their own voices, the director writes a podcast on a topic for several speakers, continues a script already begun or rewrites the lines you select, and the timeline shows every overlap.

Speech recognition with the speakers

A recording, a video or a link becomes a transcript with who said what and when: Parakeet-TDT, Nemotron 3.5 or CrisperWhisper, up to eight speakers, a karaoke player, cut by text and export to SRT, VTT, ASS, TXT or JSON.

For AI agents

While the studio is open, an agent such as Claude Code or Cursor does everything the window does over MCP: voices a text into a file, edits projects, casts voices, transcribes and checks recordings.

FAQ

What graphics card do I need?

An NVIDIA card with 8 GB of video memory or more. From Turing (GTX 16, RTX 20) with driver 580 the engines run on CUDA 13; GTX 900, GTX 10, Volta and drivers 525–579 get CUDA 12 builds. Drama 3 needs CUDA 13 and runs whole on 24 GB; from 6 GB its experts live in RAM, which then needs 32 GB. AMD and Intel cards run the Vulkan builds, a Mac runs Metal; these are not tested by the author. About 9 GB of disk for the default models and engines.

Is it free?

Yes. The studio is free and open source, and the models download for free from inside it.

Does it work offline? Are my texts sent anywhere?

Your texts and recordings are yours, and they stay on your disk.

  • No account — there is nowhere to sign up and no reason to.
  • Local by default — voicing, recognition, stress and the director run on your computer.
  • Cloud only if you want it — OpenRouter, Google Gemini, fal.ai and Runware voices are off until you add a key, and each asks before its first call.
How does it handle Russian stress?

Numbers in the right case and gender, dates, times, money and units are written out before the engine reads. Stress, «ё» and homographs are placed by RUAccent by the sentence, doubtful ones are highlighted for you, and your own marks stay.

Supertonic 3 and Qwen3-TTS place Russian stress by guess. The studio marks it before the engine reads: RUAccent picks замо́к or за́мок, му́ки or муки́ by the sentence, and numbers, dates and units are written out in their cases.

Can I use what I voice?

The studio is MIT. The models are not: each keeps its own license, listed in the README. Read them before you publish or sell what you voice. README

Can an AI agent use it?

While the studio is open, an agent such as Claude Code or Cursor does everything the window does over MCP: voices a text into a file, edits projects, casts voices, transcribes and checks recordings. Tools written for OpenAI audio work through its OpenAI-compatible API.

claude mcp add --transport http speech-studio http://127.0.0.1:8797/mcp
How is it built?

The service is compiled into the desktop app and runs Drama 3, Qwen3-TTS and transcribe.cpp in the same process through their C ABIs; Higgs, Audio8, Supertonic, Parakeet, Nemotron, CrisperWhisper and diarization run in the audio.cpp server beside it. On CUDA, Vulkan or Metal. Not a single Python process at run time.
